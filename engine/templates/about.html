About this tracker

What this is

An unofficial fan tracker of the Names of the Day posted by Carmen's Italian Ice on their public Facebook page. Every day the shop picks a name (or two — duos get both) and announces it; if it's your name, you get a free gelati. This site answers one question over the whole archive: when was a name last called — and how often?

How the data gets here

The pipeline is deliberately boring:

  1. Scrape. A small automation opens Carmen's public Facebook page once a day, reads the Names of the Day post, and saves what it saw.
  2. Commit. Each day becomes one plain-text file in this repository — data/days/2026-08-31.txt contains exactly PEREZ & LYNN. The data is human-readable, and every day is a diff you can audit in git.
  3. Build. On every push (plus a nightly rebuild), a GitHub Action runs a small static site generator over those files and renders the pages you're browsing.
  4. Publish. The finished site is deployed to GitHub Pages. No database, no tracking, no cookies — just files.

This tracker launched in {{ year }}. To make the stats meaningful from day one, we also went back through Carmen's public Facebook history and backfilled earlier Names of the Day from the original posts and photos — as far back as {{ fmt_med(days[0].date) }} — as completely as we could recover them.

A few honest caveats about that backfill:

  • Missing ≠ closed. A gray day in the archive means we couldn't recover that day's post (deleted, unavailable, or unreadable) — it does not mean the shop was closed.
  • Closures are marked. Days the shop announced a closure are recorded as closed ({{ closed_days }} so far), and they count as tracked days but never as name calls.
  • When in doubt, blank. Where a post was ambiguous, we left the day blank rather than guess a name, so the counts err on the side of accuracy.
  • Duos count. A day like "PEREZ & LYNN" credits both names, so total names called can exceed the number of tracked days.

The "never called" list

The stats page jokes about famous names that have never won the free gelati. Those are checked against a master list of {{ master_total }} first names distilled from the US Social Security Administration national baby-names dataset (public domain) — so "never called" means never in our archive, checked against a real census of names.
