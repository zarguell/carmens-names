🧮 Methodology

We look at names with 2+ calls and compute their average interval between appearances. "Overdue" means 365+ days since last call. "On the Clock" means the name has a consistent pattern (2+ calls) and is within 30 days of its expected return date.

This is fan speculation, not a crystal ball. Carmen picks names, not algorithms.
